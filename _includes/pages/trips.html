{%- comment -%} One article per trip, newest first, from `_data/trips.yml`: the date, styled like Story's, then the title and optional description. Galleries preserve each photo's full aspect ratio; a single preview uses the shared image frame. On wide screens trips sit two to a row, read left to right, so the order stays newest first. The packing is decided here, at build time: - a trip with more than one photo takes the whole row (`trip--wide`); - a single-photo trip that would sit alone in its row, because the next trip takes a whole row or there is no next trip, takes the whole row too, with its text beside its photo instead of above it (`trip--side`); - every other trip takes one column. No row is left half empty and no trip moves out of date order.
